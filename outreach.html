L’Odyssée Cosmique de Pénélope

Comic book, published by CNRS Éditions

A science comic book following Pénélope on her cosmic odyssey, as she discovers the fascinating world of research through encounters with six PhD students. Along the way, she explores their doctoral projects in cosmology and astroparticle physics at LAPP and LAPTh in Annecy, France. Blending adventure, humour, and scientific discovery, the comic offers an accessible and entertaining journey into the mysteries of the Universe and the everyday life of young researchers.

I have led the project since 2024 and co-authored the story and script.
