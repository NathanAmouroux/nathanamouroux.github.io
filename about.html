About

Cosmologist · LAPP, Annecy, France

I am a cosmologist working at the intersection of observational cosmology, astronomical instrumentation, and data analysis. My research focuses on improving the reliability of astronomical observations and developing robust methods to extract cosmological information from large imaging surveys.

During my PhD, I worked on three complementary aspects of the Vera C. Rubin Observatory's Legacy Survey of Space and Time (LSST): characterizing the telescope's photometric response using a Collimated Beam Projector (CBP), mitigating the impact of bright objects on galaxy samples, and developing methods for galaxy cluster detection and validation.

More broadly, I am interested in how we can turn increasingly large astronomical datasets into precise and reliable measurements of the Universe.
