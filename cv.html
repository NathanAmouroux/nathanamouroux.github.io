Research experience

Oct 2023 – Sep 2026
Ph.D. in CosmologyLaboratoire d’Annecy de Physique des Particules (LAPP), Annecy, France

Photometric calibration with the Collimated Beam Projector, bright object masking and galaxy cluster detection for cosmology with Rubin-LSST

  • Characterization of the Rubin telescope’s instrumental response with the Collimated Beam Projector (CBP).
  • Development of the workflow and software for the analysis of CBP data.
  • Development and implementation of the foreground-object masking strategy for the cosmological analysis of LSST data.
Advisor: Thibault Guillemin

Master 2 internshipLaboratoire d’Annecy de Physique des Particules (LAPP), Annecy, France

Study of the performance of the AMICO galaxy cluster detection algorithm for cluster selection in Rubin-LSST
